## Brand & Style

This design system expresses authoritative technical mastery, industrial discipline, and architectural clarity. Built to anchor a platform matching women and dissident trade craftspeople (electricians, builders, plumbers, carpenters, technical installers) with clients, the aesthetic explicitly rejects soft, patronizing, or decorative clichés. There are no pastel confectionery palettes, bubbly geometry, or decorative flourishes.

Instead, the design takes cues from architectural blueprints, industrial trade spec sheets, technical shop tickets, and international modernist signage. It establishes an atmosphere of undisputed competence, mechanical precision, and structural reliability. 

Visual characteristics:
- **Structural Blueprint Grid:** Layouts prioritize explicit hairline structural scaffolding over soft, floaty layers.
- **Utilitarian Density:** Tight, intentional vertical rhythms paired with generous typographic contrast.
- **Indexical Metadata:** System states, categories, and metrics are handled like trade indices and serial numbers (`[SEC-01]`, `[TRD-08]`, `REV.A`).
- **Tactile Materiality:** Warm stone substrates paired against deep technical graphite and precise violet energy accents.

## Colors

The color system operates with functional clarity, avoiding arbitrary decoration. Colors denote status, craft verification, structural delineations, and action states.

### Core Roles
- **Technical Black (`#171719`):** Primary ink for high-hierarchy typography, solid status banners, and authoritative structural frames.
- **OFICIA Violet (`#7057C7`):** High-precision instrument accent. Reserved strictly for primary interactive actions, critical verification stamps, focused input boundaries, and key status counters.
- **Stone (`#E9E5DD`):** Neutral tactile foundation. Provides a grounded, non-clinical warmth reminiscent of architectural drafting paper, stone masonry, and uncoated technical documentation.
- **Base Canvas & Crisp Light (`#FBFBF9` & `#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity primary reading canvas and card surfaces.
- **Wireframe Charcoal (`#2B2B30` / `#D5D0C6`):** Precision hairlines, division rules, structural grid lines, and technical borders.
- **Functional Semantic:** 
  - Verification & Active State: `#2E7D32` (Terminal Emerald)
  - Warning / Advisory: `#D97706` (Industrial Amber)
  - Critical / Error: `#DC2626` (Safety Crimson)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy enforces clear structural communication through three distinct, dedicated typefaces:

1. **Space Grotesk (Display & Headings):** Delivers geometric firmness, technical angularity, and architectural structure. Used for brand declarations, section markers, profile headlines, and trade category titles.
2. **Inter (Interface & Documentation):** Neutral, neutral-density grotesque engineered for high-density readability. Utilized across bios, terms, service descriptions, form inputs, and procedural instructions.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Index, Data & Micro-Labels):** Carries the technical ledger character. Used exclusively for license numbers, certification hashes, trade code identifiers (e.g., `[ELEC-01]`), timestamps, ratings, and step trackers.

All caps typography is paired with tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) and reserved for tabular headings and status designations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a modular 12-column Swiss grid system, echoing the discipline of industrial blue-lining and drafting tables.

### Layout Principles
- **Scaffold Borders & Continuous Hairlines:** Blocks connect via shared 1px boundaries (`#D5D0C6` or `#2B2B30`) rather than isolated floating cards with margins. Containers assemble like modular architectural modules.
- **Section Headers & Index Strips:** Major layout zones are heralded by a standardized index banner (`[ZONE 01 / REGION DE OPERACIONES]`), establishing orientation before content reveals.
- **Responsive Adaptation:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** Strict 12-column grid. Information-dense side panels, technical specs pinned to sticky modular rails, multi-column trade index tables.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout. Metadata collapses below primary visual documentation while maintaining perimeter hairline frames.
  - **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout. Structural gutters shrink to `1rem`. Modules stack vertically with contiguous borders, transforming cards into full-width ruled register rows.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects diffuse, blurred drop shadows and glossy skeuomorphic materials. Depth is communicated strictly through planar surface contrast and technical linework.

- **Flat Architectural Planarity:** Surfaces lie flat on the workspace. Visual priority is dictated by surface color shifts (`#FBFBF9` against `#E9E5DD` and `#171719`).
- **Precision Hairlines (1px Rule):** Hierarchy is delineated by crisp, uninterrupted borders in `#D5D0C6` (light context) or `#2B2B30` (technical dark context).
- **Hard Technical Inset:** Interactive elements (such as pressed buttons or toggle states) switch borders or adopt a solid, hard 2px offset border in `#171719` rather than ambient blurring.
- **Overlay Panels & Drawers:** Modal sheets and technical inspector panels use an opaque background with a continuous `#171719` hairline boundary and a solid technical backdrop wash (`rgba(23, 23, 25, 0.65)`).

## Shapes

The shape system adopts complete angular precision (`roundedness: 0`). Elements have sharp, 90-degree corners, mirroring drafting paper, steel stock, masonry blocks, and blueprint legends.

- **Zero Radius:** Buttons, badge indicators, cards, modular containers, modal dialogs, and text fields have `border-radius: 0px`.
- **Chamfered Corners (Selective Technical Indicator):** Primary CTA chips or trade verification stamps may feature a 4px diagonal mechanical chamfer (corner notch) to emphasize industrial utility and physical blueprint tickets.
- **Tabular Indicators:** Grouped segment controls align edge-to-edge as contiguous geometric grids.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Triggers
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#7057C7` fill, white bold Space Grotesk text, 0px radius, 1px border `#7057C7`. Hover state triggers an inversion to `#171719` with a `#7057C7` hairline accent or hard 2px right-down shadow.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background with a crisp 1px `#171719` border and `#171719` text. Hover shifts background to `#E9E5DD`.
- **Technical Utility (Ghost/Action):** Monospace font with bracket wrappers (`[ + AGREGAR CERTIFICACIÓN ]`), borderless with underline on hover.

### 2. Input Fields & Selects
- **Structure:** Squared boxes with a 1px `#D5D0C6` border and off-white `#FFFFFF` fill.
- **Labeling:** High-contrast `JetBrains Mono` micro-label positioned directly above or set into the top-left border line (`[FIELD_ID: MATRICULA]`).
- **Focus State:** 1px solid `#7057C7` border with a subtle technical bracket marker appearing at the trailing edge.

### 3. Verification Badges & Trade Tags
- **Trade Code Badges:** Small squared chips containing trade abbreviations (e.g., `ELEC-IV`, `PLMB-II`, `GAS-MAT`). Solid Stone (`#E9E5DD`) background with a 1px `#2B2B30` border and `JetBrains Mono` typography.
- **Official Seal / Dissident & Craft Verification:** High-contrast badge with `#171719` background, `#FFFFFF` mono text, and a persistent `#7057C7` live verification indicator dot (3x3px square).

### 4. Modular Trade Profile Cards
- Built as contiguous drafting blocks bounded by 1px hairlines.
- Divided into three systematic zones:
  1. *Top Rail:* Monospace index code (`#0412`), status badge, and trade category.
  2. *Core Canvas:* Full name, technical license number, years of validated craft experience, and core specialty tags.
  3. *Base Action Bar:* Fixed price index or quotation status with direct terminal action trigger (`VER EXPEDIENTE / CONTACTAR`).

### 5. Checkboxes, Radios, & Switches
- **Checkboxes:** Squared 16x16px boxes with a 1px border. Checked state displays a solid `#171719` or `#7057C7` fill containing a sharp technical cross (`×`) or geometric square (`■`), avoiding standard curved ticks.
- **Switches:** Squared dual-state slider with zero border radius. Active state illuminates in `#7057C7`.

### 6. Technical Inspection Accordion / Matrices
- Structured data tables with thin continuous horizontal rules.
- Headers are styled in uppercase `label-caps` with subtle background tint `#E9E5DD`.
- Numerical indices, coordinate stamps, and portfolio metric counters are set strictly in `JetBrains Mono`.
